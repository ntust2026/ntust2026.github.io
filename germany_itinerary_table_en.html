Travel Notes
🌤 Late May Weather
  • Higher chance of rain (especially in mountain areas) — please carry a light rain jacket or waterproof外套.
  • Long daylight hours in late May; sunset around 21:00.
🚗 Transport & Car Rental
  • Must bring Taiwan driver's license + International Driving Permit (Xiaohui in charge).
  • BMW, Audi & Benz tours are pre-booked; Germans are punctual. Please arrive 15 minutes early; late arrival may result in cancellation without refund.
🏠 Local Information
  • Voltage 230V, Type C/F outlets. Verify your hair dryer/styling tools support international voltage.
  • Recommend bringing a 10,000mAh+ power bank (long drives + navigation drain battery).
  • Sundays (5/24, 5/31): All supermarkets & shops closed — stock up on essentials beforehand.
  • Ingolstadt Village: spend threshold for tax refund; bring passport copy as backup; tax refund also available at airport.
🧂 Eagle's Nest & Salt Mine
  • Underground tunnels are a constant 12°C with high humidity. Protective suits are provided, but wear warm long-sleeve or sweater underneath.
  • Ground is wet and uneven; no flip-flops allowed. Wear sturdy athletic shoes with good grip.
🏎️ Factory Tour Dress Code (BMW · Audi · Benz)
Dress Code (Important)
  • Shoes: Must wear fully enclosed flat shoes (athletic shoes recommended). Flip-flops, sandals, heels, or open-toe shoes are strictly prohibited; improper footwear may result in denied entry.
  • Clothing: Long pants recommended to avoid bumping into walkways or catching on equipment.
Photography Policy
  • No photography or video recording in factory production line areas.
  • Guides will typically require phones and cameras to be locked in lockers or sealed bags before entry.
